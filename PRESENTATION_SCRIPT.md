**[Demo flow - describe visually]**

## Potential Investor Questions & Suggested Answers

### Q: "What's your customer acquisition cost?"

**A:** "Great question. We're targeting $500-$1,500 CAC for law firm customers, with LTV of $5,000-$15,000 based on subscription and report usage. For individual attorneys, we're targeting $100-$300 CAC with $1,200-$2,400 LTV. Our primary channels are direct sales, legal tech conferences, content marketing, and referrals—which we expect to be strong given the value proposition."

### Q: "How do you compete with established players like LexisNexis?"

**A:** "We're not competing head-on with enterprise players. We're targeting the mid-market—firms that need enterprise-quality analysis but can't afford $10,000+ annual licenses. Our advantages: 50-70% lower cost, modern UX, faster results, and our unique AI chatbot feature. We're also more agile—we can iterate and improve faster than legacy systems."

### Q: "What's your defensibility? What stops a competitor from copying this?"

**A:** "Three things: First, our multi-model report system with confidence scoring is proprietary—it's not just using an API, it's our methodology. Second, network effects—more patents in our database means better analysis, which attracts more users. Third, data moat—user queries improve our chatbot, creating a feedback loop. Plus, we're moving fast—by the time competitors catch up, we'll have market leadership."

### Q: "What are the risks?"

**A:** "Key risks: First, customer acquisition—legal is a relationship-driven industry, so we need strong sales execution. Second, AI API costs—we're monitoring this, but our margins are strong even with current costs. Third, regulatory—patent analysis is sensitive, so we need to ensure compliance. We're addressing all of these: building a sales team, optimizing AI costs, and consulting with legal experts on compliance."

### Q: "When do you become profitable?"

**A:** "Based on our projections, we break even on operations at 18 months with $1.1M ARR. At that point, we're generating positive cash flow. By 24 months with $3.2M+ ARR, we're highly profitable with 70-80% gross margins. The key is getting to scale quickly, which is why we're raising capital now."

### Q: "What's your go-to-market strategy?"

**A:** "Three-pronged approach: First, direct sales to IP law firms—we're hiring 2-3 sales professionals to target the top 200 firms. Second, content marketing and SEO—we're creating resources that attorneys search for, driving organic leads. Third, partnerships—we're exploring integrations with legal tech platforms and patent research firms. We're also leveraging referrals—once attorneys see the value, they share it with colleagues."

### Q: "How do you handle data privacy and security?"

**A:** "Critical for legal industry. We're using enterprise-grade infrastructure—Google Cloud Platform with encryption at rest and in transit. We're implementing role-based access control, audit logging, and compliance with data protection regulations. We're also consulting with legal experts to ensure we meet industry standards. Security is not optional in this market."

## Presentation Tips

1. **Practice timing:** Aim for 10-12 minutes, leaving 8-10 minutes for Q&A
2. **Visual aids:** Use screenshots, mockups, and demos—show, don't just tell
3. **Energy:** Maintain enthusiasm, especially during revenue sections
4. **Pauses:** Use strategic pauses for emphasis, especially after big numbers
5. **Eye contact:** Engage with investors, read their reactions
6. **Flexibility:** Be ready to dive deeper on any section if questions arise
7. **Confidence:** You've built something real—show that confidence
8. **Clarity:** Avoid jargon—explain technical concepts in business terms
