# Patent Gap AI - Technical Overview for Non-Technical Staff

## What This Platform Does

Patent Gap AI is an intelligent software platform that helps patent attorneys and their clients automatically identify and monitor similar patents. Think of it as a smart assistant that reads through thousands of patent documents and alerts you when it finds patents that might conflict with or relate to your own.

---

## How It Works (In Simple Terms)

### The Big Picture

When you upload a patent document to our platform, our **artificial intelligence system** reads through it and creates a "digital fingerprint" that captures the meaning and key concepts of your patent. The system then compares this fingerprint against a database of other patents to find matches.

### The Process

1. **Document Upload**: You upload your patent documents (PDFs) through a simple web interface
2. **AI Analysis**: Our **machine learning algorithms** analyze the text and extract the important concepts, technical terms, and ideas
3. **Smart Comparison**: The system compares your patent against thousands of others in our database using **semantic analysis** (understanding meaning, not just keywords)
4. **Similarity Detection**: When similar patents are found, the system calculates a similarity percentage score
5. **Instant Alerts**: You receive real-time notifications when potential conflicts or related patents are discovered

## Key Technologies (Business-Friendly Terms)

### Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Our platform uses **advanced AI technology** similar to what powers ChatGPT and other modern AI tools. The system "learns" to understand patent language and can recognize similar concepts even when different words are used. All data is stored securely in **the cloud** (Google Cloud Platform), which means:

### Real-Time Processing

The system processes information **instantly** as new patents are added, meaning you get alerts immediately when something relevant is discovered—no waiting for overnight batch processing.

### Data Integration

Our platform connects directly with **official patent databases** (USPTO) to automatically pull in the latest patent information, ensuring you always have access to current data without manual updates.
