# Patent Gap AI - User Guide

## What is Patent Gap AI?

Patent Gap AI is an AI-powered patent management and monitoring platform designed for attorneys and innovators. It helps protect intellectual property and monitor potential infringements through automated similarity detection and smart alert systems.

---

## What Can Patent Gap AI Do?

### 1. Patent Management
- **Add New Patents**: Submit patent applications with title, description, and keywords
- **Document Upload**: Upload PDF documents via drag-and-drop functionality for patent description
- **Track Patents**: Monitor active and closed patents in your portfolio once every N days
- **View Details**: Access comprehensive case information for each patent

### 2. AI-Powered Similarity Detection
- **Automatic Analysis**: System automatically analyzes patent documents for similarity
- **Smart Comparison**: Compares new patents against existing cases in the database with regard to context
- **Similarity Scores**: Generates percentage-based similarity scores between patents
- **Related Patents**: Identifies potentially related or conflicting patents automatically

### 3. Smart Alert System (In development, not completed)
- **Real-Time Notifications**: Receive instant alerts when similar patents are detected
- **Detailed Information**: Alerts show case details, descriptions, and trigger dates
- **Quick Navigation**: Click on notifications to navigate directly to related cases
- **Personalized Alerts**: Alerts are customized based on your specific cases and relationships

### 4. Dual User Experience
- **Attorney Dashboard**: Full-featured case management with statistics, open cases, and case assignment capabilities
- **Client Dashboard**: Simplified, user-friendly view showing active and closed patents

### 5. Case Management
- **View Assigned Cases**: See all cases assigned to you (Attorney)
- **Browse Open Cases**: Explore and accept available cases from the open case pool (Attorney)
- **Track Status**: Monitor case status (Active, Pending, Completed, etc.)
- **Case Details**: View comprehensive case information including references, keywords, and timeline
- **Update Information**: Modify case status and details as needed (Attorney)

### 6. Document Management
- **Upload Documents**: Submit PDF documents for patent applications
- **Organize Files**: Store and organize patent documents efficiently
- **View Documents**: Access patent documents associated with specific cases

### 7. Profile & Account Management
- **View Profile**: See your profile with case and basic patent statistics
- **Change Password**: Update your account password securely
- **Account Information**: View account creation date and email

### 8. Demo Request System
- **Request Demonstrations**: Schedule personalized platform demonstrations
- **Flexible Scheduling**: Choose date, time, and timezone for your demo
- **Contact Submission**: Submit your contact information for follow-up

### 9. Lead Generation & Discovery
- **Discover Opportunities**: Find potential clients through patent analysis (Attorney)
- **AI Insights**: Identify opportunities based on AI-powered insights
- **Similar Cases**: Find similar cases that may need legal services (Attorney)

### 10. Patent Analysis & Insights
- **Related Patents**: View related patents with similarity scores
- **References**: See patent references and citations
- **Timeline Tracking**: Track patent timelines and status updates
- **Relationship Analysis**: Analyze relationships between different patents

## What Is Patent Gap AI Meant To Do?

Patent Gap AI is designed to:

- **Help Attorneys**: Manage patent portfolios efficiently and effectively
- **Automate Detection**: Automatically detect patent similarities without manual review
- **Early Warning System**: Provide early warnings about potential conflicts or infringements
- **Streamline Workflow**: Simplify patent case management and organization
- **Quick Discovery**: Enable rapid discovery of related patents and cases
- **Data-Driven Decisions**: Support decision-making with AI-powered insights
- **Document Organization**: Simplify patent document storage and retrieval
- **Connect Professionals**: Help attorneys connect with potential clients through patent analysis
